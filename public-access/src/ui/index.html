Show in Home Assistant

This add-on can appear in Home Assistant as a device called Public Access. You get sensors (visitors, visits, refused requests, status), a switch to pause public access, limits you can change, a switch per address, and buttons to disconnect visitors or reload the view. The connection is encrypted.
